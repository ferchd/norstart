# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Norstar to be a welcoming, respectful project for everyone. Participants are expected to act with care toward one another and to help keep collaboration constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information without consent.
- Deliberate intimidation, humiliation, or disruption of project spaces.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Include the relevant context and links. Maintainers will review reports and may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to the situation.

## Attribution

This policy is original to Norstar and was informed by common open-source community standards.
